## 🧩 About

**Rust Config** is a comprehensive mod menu for Rust featuring overlay, ESP, aimbot, loot tracker, radar, and configurable settings.

Based on popular mods like **Rust Hack Menu**, **Rust Aimbot Menu**, and **Rust ESP Menu**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- Overlay — customizable in-game HUD
- Aimbot — adjustable FOV and smoothness
- ESP — player, loot, and container outlines
- Radar — minimap with enemy positions
- Loot Tracker — highlight valuable items

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- Skin Unlocker — access all skins
